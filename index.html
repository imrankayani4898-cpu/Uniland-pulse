Help

Key terms in plain English

Off-planBought from the developer before it's built, paid in instalments.
Secondary / readyA finished home bought from its current owner.
DLDDubai Land Department — registers every sale.
OqoodDLD registration for off-plan units; also 4%.
AED / sq ftPrice ÷ size. The fairest way to compare.
20/40/4020% booking, 40% while building, 40% at handover.
Gross yieldYearly rent ÷ price. Net also deducts charges.
Service chargeYearly building upkeep, per sq ft.
Golden Visa10-year residency for property of AED 2M+.
Makani10-digit official address of any building.

Sources: DLD public counter and releases, DXBinteract, Property Finder, Bayut, ValuStrat, Westgate, research houses. Calculator results are estimates, not financial advice.
